Exclude Igareck AntiDPI from v2rayNG

1. Open the side menu ☰ → “Per-app proxy”.

2. Enable “Per-app proxy” and “Bypass”, find Igareck AntiDPI and select it.
The switches may be called “Enable per-app proxy” and “Bypass mode”, depending on the version.

Igareck AntiDPI is now excluded from the v2rayNG VPN tunnel.

Add the local SOCKS5 profile

1. Open the side menu ☰ → “Groups” → “+”.
Create a “Local profiles” group and leave its URL blank.

2. On the main screen, select the “Local profiles” tab and tap “+” → “Add SOCKS”.
Enter Igareck_AntiDPI_Local as the name, 127.0.0.1 as the address and @PORT@ as the port.
Leave the username and password blank and save the profile.

Access websites through a VPN

1. Open the side menu ☰ → “Groups”.

On the “Groups” screen, tap the pencil beside your VPN subscription.
In “Front proxy”, select Igareck_AntiDPI_Local or enter its exact name.
Leave “Landing proxy” blank. Save the settings.

2. Return to the subscription tab.
In the ⋮ menu, choose “Test real delay”, select a working VPN profile and connect.
v2rayNG connects to it through the local Igareck SOCKS5 proxy.

Access websites directly

1. Disconnect your current profile. On the “Local profiles” tab, select Igareck_AntiDPI_Local and tap the connect button. To route app traffic, open “Settings” → “Mode settings” → “Mode” → “VPN”.

2. Open the website you want (YouTube, Discord, LinkedIn or others), or use an app such as Discord.
v2rayNG sends its traffic through the local Igareck SOCKS5 proxy for a direct connection to the website.

For Discord voice, enable UDP over SOCKS and Discord/STUN in AntiDPI.
