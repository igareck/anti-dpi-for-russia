1. Enable Android Developer options.

2. Connect to any available Wi-Fi access point, with or without internet access, and activate the network module. You can also connect to a computer via USB.

3. Choose a strategy and tap “Start / Apply” (the round button).

4. Configure your VPN client using the instructions below.
