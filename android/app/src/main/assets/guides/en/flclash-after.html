Access websites directly

1. Open “Profiles” and select the local YAML profile you imported from “Save YAML for FlClash” above.

2. On the “Dashboard”, select “Rule” under “Outbound mode”.
If FlClash is stopped, tap “Start”.

3. Open the website or app you want.
Igareck_AntiDPI_Local sends the traffic received by FlClash through the local Igareck SOCKS5 proxy directly to the website.

For Discord voice, enable UDP over SOCKS and Discord/STUN in AntiDPI.
