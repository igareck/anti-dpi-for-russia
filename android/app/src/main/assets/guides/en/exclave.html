Exclude Igareck AntiDPI from Exclave

1. Open the side menu ☰ → “Settings”.
Find “Route settings” and tap “Per-app VPN mode”.

2. Select “Bypass” at the top, find Igareck AntiDPI and select it.
Igareck AntiDPI is now excluded from the Exclave VPN tunnel.

Add the local SOCKS5 profile

Open the side menu ☰ → “Configuration”.

On the “Configuration” screen, tap → “Manual settings” → “Other” → “SOCKS”.
Enter Igareck_AntiDPI_Local as the name, 127.0.0.1 as the server, @PORT@ as the port and SOCKS5 as the version.
Leave the username and password blank, then tap the “Apply” check mark.

Access websites through a VPN

1. Open the side menu ☰ → “Groups”.

On the “Groups” screen, tap the pencil beside your VPN subscription.
Open “Front proxy” → “Select profile…” and choose Igareck_AntiDPI_Local.
Leave “Landing proxy” set to “Disabled”.
Save the group settings with the “Apply” check mark.

2. Return to the subscription or balancer tab (☰ → “Configuration”).

3. If you already have a balancer and want to use it:

On the “Configuration” screen, open the local group that contains the balancer.
This is usually “Ungrouped”.
Tap the pencil beside the balancer to open its settings.
Enable “Use group front proxy”.
Save the settings with the “Apply” check mark.
Select the balancer and connect.

4. To choose a profile in the subscription manually:

Tap the subscription name at the top.
Open ⋮ → “Connection test” → “URL test” and wait for the test to finish.
Select a working VPN server and connect.
Exclave connects to it through the local Igareck SOCKS5 proxy.

Access websites directly

1. Open the side menu ☰ → “Configuration”.

On the “Configuration” screen, open the local group where you saved the SOCKS5 profile.
This is usually “Ungrouped”.
Select Igareck_AntiDPI_Local and connect.

2. Open the website you want (YouTube, Discord, LinkedIn or others), or use an app such as Discord.
Exclave sends its traffic through the local Igareck SOCKS5 proxy for a direct connection to the website.

For Discord voice, enable UDP over SOCKS and Discord/STUN in AntiDPI.
